## 🎙️ Recording & Voiceover Quick Reference Guide

### Pacing & Word Count Target
- **Total Word Count:** ~680 words across 5 minutes (~136 words/minute).
- **Natural Cadence:** Speak at a comfortable, warm, conversational speed. Do not rush technical acronyms (say *"U-D-I-S-E Plus"*, *"D-B-T"*, *"A-P-A-A-R"* cleanly).
- **Pause Points:** Leave a 1.5-second visual pause at every scene transition (marked with ⏱️) so the viewer can absorb the UI screens.

### Recommended Screen Recording Resolutions
- **Resolution:** 1080p (1920 × 1080) or 4K.
- **Zoom Factor:** 100% or 110% in Chrome / Arc for clear, legible text.
- **Mouse Highlight:** Enable yellow click halos or cursor magnification for smooth visual tracking.
